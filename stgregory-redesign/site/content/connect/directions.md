## Address

**13407 Roxbury Rd, Silver Spring, MD 20904**

The church is also accessible from **800 E. Randolph Rd**.

## By Car

From the Capital Beltway (I-495), take the exit for US-29 (Colesville Rd) toward Silver Spring. Follow Randolph Rd east; the church is near the intersection with Roxbury Rd.

Free parking is available on site.

## By Transit

The church is accessible via Montgomery County Ride On bus routes. The nearest Metro station is Silver Spring (Red Line), approximately 4 miles west.

The **Ordo** provides the official daily calendar and lectionary for Western Rite Orthodox parishes, indicating the proper texts, readings, and observances for each day of the liturgical year.

## Current Year Ordo

The Ordo for the current liturgical year is available from the Western Rite Vicariate. Contact the parish office for a copy, or consult the resources below.

## How to Use the Ordo

The Ordo is used in conjunction with the **Orthodox Missal** and the **Antiphoner** to determine:

- The rank and color of each day's observance
- The Proper prayers (Introit, Collect, Epistle, Gradual, Gospel, etc.)
- The Office hymns and antiphons
- Feast days, fasts, and commemorations

## Saints of the Day

The Western Rite calendar includes saints venerated by both East and West, reflecting the undivided heritage of the ancient Church. Many of the saints commemorated — from the Apostles and Martyrs to the great Doctors and Monastics — are honored across all Orthodox traditions.

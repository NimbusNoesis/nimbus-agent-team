The Daily Office — also called the Liturgy of the Hours or the Divine Office — is the ancient pattern of daily prayer inherited from the monastic tradition of the Western Church. Through psalms, hymns, Scripture readings, and prayers, the Office sanctifies the hours of the day and unites the individual Christian with the prayer of the whole Church.

## The Hours

- **Matins** (Morning Prayer) — The principal morning office
- **Lauds** — Praise at dawn
- **Prime, Terce, Sext, None** — The "little hours" throughout the day
- **Vespers** (Evening Prayer) — The principal evening office
- **Compline** (Night Prayer) — Prayer before sleep

## Texts & Resources

The Antiphoner, Office Hymns, and Scripture lessons for the Daily Office can be found in the parish's liturgical resources. The annual [Ordo](/worship/ordo/) provides the calendar governing which texts are used on each day.

## Praying at Home

The Daily Office is not only for clergy and monastics — laypeople are encouraged to pray at least Matins and Vespers daily. Contact the parish for guidance on beginning a rule of prayer.

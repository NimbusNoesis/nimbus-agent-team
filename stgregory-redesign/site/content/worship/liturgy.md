The principal worship of St. Gregory's parish is the **Mass (Divine Liturgy) according to the Rite of St. Gregory the Great** — the historic liturgy of the Western Church, now celebrated within the Orthodox faith.

## History

The Liturgy of St. Gregory has its roots in the worship of the early Roman Church. Codified and enriched by Pope St. Gregory the Great in the sixth century, this liturgical form served as the foundation of Western Christian worship for over a millennium.

With the establishment of the Western Rite Vicariate within the Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese, this ancient rite was restored for use in Orthodox parishes, allowing Western Christians to enter the fullness of Orthodox faith without abandoning the liturgical heritage of their ancestors.

## Structure of the Mass

The Mass follows the traditional Western shape:

1. **The Preparation** — Prayers at the foot of the altar
2. **The Liturgy of the Word** — Introit, Kyrie, Gloria, Collect, Epistle, Gradual, Gospel, Sermon, Creed
3. **The Liturgy of the Eucharist** — Offertory, Preface, Canon, Communion
4. **The Dismissal** — Post-communion prayer, blessing, Last Gospel

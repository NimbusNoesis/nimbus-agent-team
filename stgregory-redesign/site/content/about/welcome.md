## What to Expect

Whether you are Orthodox, coming from another Christian tradition, or simply curious about the faith, you are welcome at St. Gregory's. Our services follow the ancient Western liturgical forms — if you have attended a traditional Catholic or Anglican service, much will feel familiar.

## Service Schedule

- **Vespers** — Saturday at 6:00 PM
- **Matins** — Sunday at 9:00 AM
- **Divine Liturgy (Mass)** — Sunday at 9:30 AM

Holy day services are announced in the weekly bulletin and on our calendar.

## Location

We worship at **13407 Roxbury Rd, Silver Spring, MD 20904**, conveniently located near the Capital Beltway. [Get directions →](/connect/directions/)

## What Should I Wear?

There is no formal dress code. Most parishioners dress in business casual or "Sunday best," but we welcome you however you come.

## Can I Receive Communion?

Holy Communion is reserved for baptized and chrismated Orthodox Christians who have prepared through prayer, fasting, and recent confession. All are welcome to come forward for a blessing at the end of the service.

## Questions?

Please don't hesitate to reach out. Fr. Nicholas and the parish community are happy to answer any questions. [Contact us →](/connect/contact/)

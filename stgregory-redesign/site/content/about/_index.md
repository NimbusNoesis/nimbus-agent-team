St. Gregory the Great Orthodox Church is a Western Rite congregation of the Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese of North America, part of the Greek Orthodox Patriarchate of Antioch and all the East — one of the oldest Christian communities in the world, tracing its origins to the Apostles Peter and Paul.

As a Western Rite parish, we celebrate the ancient liturgical forms of the Christian West that have been restored to Orthodox use. Our principal service is the Mass (Divine Liturgy) according to the Rite of St. Gregory the Great.

## Fr. Nicholas Richard Alford — Pastor

Fr. Nicholas serves as the pastor of St. Gregory the Great. He and his wife, Khouria Rebecca, came to Orthodoxy in 1995, having previously served in the Episcopal Church. Fr. Nicholas brings a deep love for the Western liturgical tradition and a pastoral heart for welcoming seekers and inquirers into the Orthodox faith.

**Contact:** [frnicholas@aol.com](mailto:frnicholas@aol.com)

## Fr. Jeffrey Raphael Garcia — Assistant Pastor

Fr. Jeffrey serves as the assistant pastor of St. Gregory's. A former Roman Catholic priest who served parishes in the Philippines and Virginia, Fr. Jeffrey became Orthodox in 2010. He brings a rich pastoral and liturgical experience to the parish.
